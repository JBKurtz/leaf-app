What you're testing

No camera. On purpose.

Leaf doesn't point a lens and guess like other apps. You observe the plant and match its traits in the app, narrowing the nearby plants until a handful remain, then make the final call yourself with photos and descriptions. It's a little more work, and it isn't for everyone, but it's intentional: Leaf asks you to really look at a plant, and rewards you as you move deeper into nature. Getting that difference right is exactly where your feedback matters most.

  • ✓ You observe traits; Leaf narrows the field to nearby matches
  • ✓ Rich profiles tell each plant's story, uses, and where it grows
  • ✓ The plants offered are based on your watershed and elevation
  • ✓ Identifying plants and reading profiles will always be free

What I'm looking for

Honest feedback, above all.

What works, what doesn't land, what breaks? Any and all feedback is welcome, in the app, through a short form, or just email me. Right now I'm especially curious about a handful of things.

  • ✓ Does Leaf start you with the right set of traits, and which would you cut?
  • ✓ Where does trait selection break down or stop narrowing well?
  • ✓ Is the trait selection screen easy to move through?
  • ✓ Are the plant profiles compelling to read?
  • ✓ Are the glossary explanations enough when you need them?
  • ✓ Anything that behaves inconsistently, or plainly breaks?
What Leaf supports today

California, and growing every day.

Leaf draws its traits and facts from authoritative floras around the world. It supports over 8,500 California native and introduced plants right now, with more added daily toward all of the roughly 23,000 you can observe in the state. It is strongest on native plants and weakest on houseplants for now.

Supported

Broadleaf trees Conifers Vines Shrubs Herbaceous Wildflowers & forbs

Not supported yet

Cacti Succulents Grasses Ferns Mosses

Good to know

A few common questions.

What do I need to take part?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later, and Apple's free TestFlight app. Leaf works best outdoors, where you can look closely at a real plant.

Does it cost anything?

No. The beta is free, and the core of Leaf, identifying plants and reading their profiles, will always be free to every user.

How do I give feedback?

In the app, through a short form, or just email me. Some weeks I might ask you to focus on a particular area or type of plant; otherwise, use Leaf whenever you are curious about a plant. Participation is voluntary and you can stop any time.

When does the open beta start?

Soon. We're finishing the closed phase first. Register now and you'll get an invitation as spots open, in the order people signed up.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to use Leaf. Registering for the beta simply puts you on the invite list.
